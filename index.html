A Perfect
Day

The kind of day that begins slowly, smells like fresh coffee, has nowhere I desperately need to be, and ends with that happy feeling of wishing the day could last just a little longer.

It starts quietly...

Morning light slips through the curtains and spreads across the room. I hear the soft sounds of the house waking up and smell breakfast being made. There is no rush. I can sit by the window, enjoy my morning and let the day begin naturally.

The day unfolds...

9:00 AM

A slow morning

A warm breakfast, sunlight on the table and a peaceful start before the rest of the world gets busy.

11:30 AM

Let's go somewhere

A little trip with my family, music playing, windows open and plenty of laughter along the way.

2:00 PM

A peaceful afternoon

Somewhere surrounded by trees and fresh air. I would explore, take pictures and enjoy not having to look at the clock every five minutes.

5:30 PM

When the sky turns gold

The sun begins to set and everything becomes soft and golden. It is the sort of moment that makes everyone stop talking for a while.

8:30 PM

A cosy ending

Favourite food, a funny movie, comfortable clothes and a warm blanket. Nothing fancy — just happy.

“Maybe a perfect day is not a day where everything goes perfectly. Maybe it is simply a day you wish you could keep forever.”
